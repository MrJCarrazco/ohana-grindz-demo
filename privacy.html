Privacy notice

This notice covers the Digital Consulting Studios design preview for 'Ohana Grindz, not the business’s official website or its separate customer records. Updated October 19, 2026.

Disabled demonstration form

The homepage form’s inputs and submit button are disabled. Its submission handler also cancels submission. It does not send an inquiry, create a booking or save a submission in a demo database. Do not enter personal or sensitive information. Calling a listed number contacts the business outside this website.

Hosting and connection information

GitHub Pages serves these static pages. GitHub and its infrastructure can process ordinary request information, including IP addresses, requested URLs, timestamps and browser headers, to deliver and secure hosting. This is distinct from an installed visitor-tracking tool. GitHub privacy statement.

External resources and links

The homepage loads Fraunces and Sora through Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Font requests disclose your IP address and request headers to Google. Google Fonts privacy information.

The homepage includes a lazy-loaded OpenStreetMap map. When it loads, your browser requests the map document and tiles from external providers, revealing connection information such as your IP address and browser headers. The map is a separate document and can have its own storage and privacy behavior. OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

DoorDash and Uber Eats links open external ordering/menu sites. Those providers handle any account, order, payment or tracking on their own services; this demo does not receive an order through those links.

Cookies, localStorage and analytics

The demo’s own code does not set cookies or use localStorage, and no custom analytics script, advertising pixel or marketing tracker is installed in this version. This does not mean that hosting produces no logs or that third-party services process no data. Browser caching and external-provider storage are separate from the demo’s own code.

Use, sharing and retention

Resources are requested to display the preview and, where present, fonts and maps. There is no active form endpoint, marketing list or submission database in this demo. Hosting and external-provider logs follow those providers’ practices; this notice does not promise a retention period or deletion of their logs. Messages you send separately by email or phone are outside the disabled form. Ask the recipient about handling and retention before sending sensitive information.
